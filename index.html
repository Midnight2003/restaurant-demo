A room for long dinners.

Our menu follows the market, not the calendar. Come early, stay late, let the table do the rest. Fire meets skill here.

Fire, salt, and smoke.

We temper heat with salt and smoke. Each pass over coals transforms texture. Our chefs paint with fire, building depth.

From sea to ember.

Fresh catches meet fire by evening. Smoke carries the ocean into every bite. We source sustainably from local fishermen.

Nightly rituals.

Lanterns glow, embers flare, service becomes music. We tend fire and guests alike. Each course arrives warm and alive.

The grill's geometry.

Cuts char on edges, hold juice at center. We plate for balance and harmony. Fire, blade, and wood create art.

Sweet endings by the flame.

Caramelized fruit, toasted nuts, gentle smoke. The fire closes with sweetness. Leave room for the memory you'll take.
